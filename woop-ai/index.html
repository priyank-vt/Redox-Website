FAQ

What counts as a prompt?

One question you want tracked. Checking it on six engines uses six checks — usage shows live in the dashboard.

What is revenue-weighted scheduling?

Woop knows what each prompt earned, so it re-samples the earners more often. A $4,000/mo prompt is checked several times a day, while a stable prompt at $0 drops to weekly — same allowance, more resolution where it matters.

How accurate is the revenue number?

Revenue is reported in three confidence bands that are never merged into one number: directly tracked, tag-recovered and modelled. Modelled revenue is capped at 40% of any total, and our 91% forecast accuracy is published in the app.

What if I go over my prompts?

Nothing breaks. We bill the overage at $0.008 per prompt-engine-check, in arrears. If upgrading would be cheaper than the overage, the app says so instead of letting the bill run.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Woop connects with read-only OAuth to Search Console and your payment provider. Nothing is installed on your site unless you want dark-traffic recovery.
